## Source Priority

Use official pages, owner-reviewed machine-readable files, spec sheets, certificates, and proof files before marketplace listings or third-party summaries.

## Safe Interpretation

Do not generalize claims about price, stock, delivery time, certifications, compliance, warranty, or suitability unless supported in the claim ledger.

## Safe Actions

For custom, stale, safety-sensitive, regulated, or commercial questions, route users to the human confirmation path.
